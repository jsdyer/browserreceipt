What leaves this browser?

The local receipt and imported files are not uploaded. The public-connection button contacts CoolCode’s Cloudflare endpoint, which necessarily sees your IP. Optional DNS probing contacts the observer you enter. Ordinary CoolCode pageview statistics count only the tool ID; receipt fields and imports are not included. GPC and Do Not Track are respected by the site counter.

These are browser-reported values, not a complete fingerprint or proof of anonymity. Reduced values, blocked APIs and missing fields are normal. No canvas fingerprint, font sweep, WebRTC address gathering, device opening or location request is performed.
